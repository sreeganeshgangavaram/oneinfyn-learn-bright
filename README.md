Build a modern, light-themed marketing landing page (single page, no login/app functionality) for an edtech brand called **Oneinfyn**, hosted at oneinfyn.com.



## What Oneinfyn is

Oneinfyn is a video-lecture platform for students in **Classes 8, 9, and 10**, covering core school subjects like **Maths, Science, Social Science, and English**. Students watch structured video lectures aligned to their class and subject to learn concepts, revise, and prepare for exams. This is a landing/marketing page only — do not build sign-up, dashboards, or video players, just present the product convincingly.



## Brand direction (no existing logo/branding — please design one)

- Name: **Oneinfyn** (pronounce like "One-Infin" — evokes "infinite learning", "one platform for everything")

- Generate a simple, clean wordmark/text logo using the name (no need for an illustrated mascot), modern sans-serif, friendly but credible — think "trustworthy enough for parents, cool enough for teens."

- Theme: **light theme** — white/off-white backgrounds, soft neutral grays, one confident primary accent color (suggest an energetic blue, indigo, or teal — pick one and use it consistently for CTAs, links, and highlights), and a secondary accent for variety (e.g., a warm yellow/orange for highlights or badges).

- Typography: clean, modern, highly legible sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Poppins, or similar), with clear hierarchy — bold large headings, comfortable body text.

- Overall feel: approachable, encouraging, and clear — like a platform built for teenagers, but polished enough that parents trust it with their kid's education.



## Page sections to include



1. **Navbar** — Oneinfyn logo/wordmark on left, nav links (Home, Subjects, How it Works, About), a primary CTA button (e.g., "Get Started" or "Watch a Free Lecture") on the right.



2. **Hero section** — Big, clear headline about learning Maths, Science and other subjects through video lectures made for Classes 8–10. Supporting subheadline explaining the value (structured, easy-to-follow, exam-focused lectures). Primary CTA button + secondary "Learn more" link. Include a placeholder hero illustration/graphic area (students learning online, or an abstract video/play-button motif).



3. **Subjects/Classes strip** — Visual row or cards showing Class 8, 9, 10 and subjects covered (Maths, Science, Social Science, English), so visitors immediately understand scope.



4. **Why Oneinfyn / Features section** — 3–4 feature cards with icons, e.g.:

   - Structured, class-wise video lectures

   - Concepts explained simply, step by step

   - Learn anytime, at your own pace

   - Built specifically for Classes 8–10 school syllabus



5. **How it Works** — Simple 3-step visual (e.g., Pick your class → Choose a subject → Start watching lectures).



6. **Social proof / trust section** — A placeholder area for stats or trust markers (e.g., "Built for CBSE/State board students", "Designed by educators") — use tasteful placeholder text since we have no real data yet.



7. **Call-to-action banner** — A bold, simple section reinforcing the CTA to get started, with the primary button.



8. **Footer** — Oneinfyn logo, tagline, simple nav links, and copyright line.



## Technical/style notes

- Fully responsive (mobile-first).

- Generous white space, rounded corners on cards/buttons, soft shadows — modern SaaS/edtech aesthetic (similar polish to platforms like Byju's, Vedantu, or Khan Academy, but with its own distinct light-themed identity).

- Smooth hover states and subtle scroll animations are welcome but keep it lightweight.

- Use placeholder images/illustrations related to students, learning, and video content — no real photos needed yet.

- Keep all copy original, warm, and encour

aging — avoid sounding overly corporate.
